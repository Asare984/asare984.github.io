Week 1 — Overview of Marketing 1

Define marketing and explain why it is more than advertising and selling.

What a good answer includes
  • Give the AMA or CIM definition
  • Show marketing covers research, product, price, place, promotion and relationships
  • Explain advertising and selling as only parts of marketing
  • Use an example, such as a mobile phone company

Explain needs, wants and demands, and why marketing myopia is dangerous.

What a good answer includes
  • Define each of the three terms with an example
  • Link demand to buying power
  • Define marketing myopia
  • Show how a firm that ignores underlying needs loses customers

Week 2 — Overview of Marketing 2

Compare the production, product, selling, marketing and societal marketing concepts.

What a good answer includes
  • State each concept in one sentence
  • Give a short example for each
  • Explain why the marketing concept starts from the customer
  • Explain what the societal concept adds

Describe the marketing mix and explain how its elements work together.

What a good answer includes
  • List the 4 Ps and define each
  • Explain the integrated marketing program
  • Show how a change in one P affects the others
  • Illustrate with a Ghanaian product

Week 3 — The Marketing Environment

Distinguish between the micro- and macro-environment and discuss three micro factors.

What a good answer includes
  • Define both environments
  • List the micro factors
  • Discuss three with how each affects marketing
  • Give an example

Discuss how the macro-environment can create opportunities and threats for a Ghanaian business.

What a good answer includes
  • List the six macro forces
  • Explain two or three in detail
  • Show an opportunity and a threat
  • Explain how firms respond

Week 5 — Understanding Consumer Behaviour

Explain the five stages of the consumer decision process using a purchase such as a smartphone.

What a good answer includes
  • Name the five stages in order
  • Explain each stage
  • Use a practical example
  • Show how marketers can influence each stage

Discuss the factors that influence consumer behaviour.

What a good answer includes
  • Group them into personal, psychological and social factors
  • Explain at least two in each group
  • Give examples
  • Explain implications for marketers

Week 6 — Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning

Explain the bases for segmenting consumer markets with examples.

What a good answer includes
  • Name the four bases
  • Give variables for each
  • Use a Ghanaian product
  • Explain the conditions for effective segmentation

Discuss the four target-market strategies.

What a good answer includes
  • Define each strategy
  • State when each suits a firm
  • Give advantages and limits
  • Conclude on how a firm chooses

Week 8 — Setting Product Strategy

Describe the stages of the product life cycle and the marketing strategies for each.

What a good answer includes
  • Define the PLC
  • Describe the four stages with sales and profits
  • Suggest marketing actions per stage
  • Mention extending the life cycle

Explain the new product development process and why new products fail.

What a good answer includes
  • List the six stages in order
  • Explain each briefly
  • Give reasons for failure
  • Give an example

Week 9 — Pricing Strategies and Programmes

Discuss the factors that influence pricing decisions.

What a good answer includes
  • Separate internal and external factors
  • Explain at least three of each
  • Give examples
  • Conclude on how a firm balances them

Explain skimming and penetration pricing and when each is suitable.

What a good answer includes
  • Define both
  • State conditions for each
  • Compare advantages and risks
  • Give examples

Week 10 — Place and Marketing Channels

Explain the role of marketing intermediaries and the benefits they provide.

What a good answer includes
  • Define a channel and intermediary
  • Explain four benefits
  • Describe types of intermediaries
  • Give examples

Discuss the types of vertical marketing system.

What a good answer includes
  • Define a VMS
  • Explain corporate, administered and contractual
  • Compare control and cost
  • Give an example of each

Week 11 — Promotion

Describe the elements of the promotional mix.

What a good answer includes
  • List the four elements
  • Define each
  • Give advantages and limits
  • Give examples

Explain the steps in the personal selling process.

What a good answer includes
  • List the seven steps in order
  • Explain each
  • Show how objections are handled
  • Illustrate with an example

Week 12 — Building and Maintaining Customer Relationships

Explain why customer relationship management is important to a firm.

What a good answer includes
  • Define CRM
  • Explain value and satisfaction
  • Explain loyalty and retention
  • Explain lifetime value and customer equity

Discuss strategies a firm can use to build customer relationships.

What a good answer includes
  • Name key strategies
  • Explain the role of technology
  • Give examples
  • Mention benefits and limits
