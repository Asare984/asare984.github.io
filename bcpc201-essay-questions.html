Week 1 — What Is an Information System?

Describe the components of an information system.

What a good answer includes
  • Name hardware, software, data, people and process
  • Define each
  • Show how they work together
  • Give an example of a business system

Trace the eras of business computing from the mainframe to the Internet of Things.

What a good answer includes
  • List the eras in order
  • Give the key technology of each
  • Explain how business use changed
  • Give dates where known
